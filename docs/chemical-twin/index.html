In KOH alkaline solution, soluble Al(OH)₄⁻ forms first. During operation, part remains dissolved and part precipitates as Al(OH)₃; therefore the overall reaction and the ≤3.32 kg product value are shown as a simplified solid-product equivalent. All liquid and solid products remain inside the spent cartridge. H₂, warm humid air and vapor leave only through the dedicated external vent; the absolute H₂ design rate remains TBD.
